Complete Documentation & Guide

Everything you need to know about using LuckyGuy on Windows.

1. First-Time Setup Wizard

When you launch LuckyGuy on a new PC, the Setup Wizard launches automatically:

  • Step 1 (Welcome): Highlights features and verifies your audio system.
  • Step 2 (Music Directory): Lets you specify your default library path (defaults to C:\Users\<You>\Music) and enables automatic genre subfolder organization.
  • Step 3 (Theme Style): Choose between Light Mode and Obsidian Dark.
  • Step 4 (Completion): Saves your preferences and opens your ready-to-use music library.

You can re-trigger the Setup Wizard anytime from Settings → Downloads & Storage → Launch Setup Wizard.

2. How Downloading Works

Simply paste any media stream link or playlist URL into the search bar:

  • Spotify Playlists & Tracks: Paste any open.spotify.com/playlist/... link. LuckyGuy extracts all 200+ tracks instantly, fetches genuine song artwork, and downloads them in an orderly multi-worker queue.
  • YouTube Audio & Videos: Paste any YouTube video or Shorts link to extract high-bitrate audio.
  • JioSaavn Tracks: Enter song titles or artist names for 320kbps Indian & International audio masters.
  • Infinite Table: Queue hundreds of tracks without UI lag or container boundaries.

3. Automatic Genre Folder Organization

Unlike conventional downloaders that dump loose audio files into your Music root, LuckyGuy organizes songs into neat directories:

  • C:\Users\<You>\Music\Bollywood\ — Bollywood & Hindi cinematic tracks
  • C:\Users\<You>\Music\Pop\ — Global Pop releases
  • C:\Users\<You>\Music\Rock\ — Classic & Alternative Rock
  • C:\Users\<You>\Music\Hip-Hop\ — Rap and Urban audio
  • C:\Users\<You>\Music\Electronic\ — EDM, Synthwave, Dance
  • C:\Users\<You>\Music\K-Pop\ — Korean Pop

Every folder also generates a folder.jpg so Windows File Explorer displays album art for the entire folder.

4. Settings & Advanced Customization

Accessible from the bottom-left gear icon in the app:

  • Spotify Credentials: While built-in Pathfinder extraction works out of the box, you can enter custom Spotify developer Client ID / Secret or browser sp_dc cookies in Settings → Plugins.
  • Default Bitrate: Set quality presets (320kbps, 256kbps, 192kbps).
  • Check for Updates: One-click version verify against GitHub Releases.
  • Sleep Timer: Set automated playback shutdown timers (15m, 30m, 60m).
